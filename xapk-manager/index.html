1. Overview

XAPK Manager is an Android utility designed to help users work with application packages and related files. Depending on the features available in your version of the app, this may include viewing apps installed on the device, extracting or backing up application packages, managing APK/XAPK-related files, viewing package information, sharing files, or starting package installation.

XAPK Manager does not require you to create an account to use its core functionality.

2. Information and data

The app may access information that is necessary to provide its features, such as:

  • information about applications and packages available on your device;
  • application names, package names, versions, icons, and related technical metadata;
  • APK, XAPK, APKS, APKM, or other files that you choose to open, manage, extract, save, or share;
  • device storage locations that you explicitly select through Android's system file picker or permissions;
  • basic technical information needed for app stability, diagnostics, advertising, or analytics if such services are enabled in the app.

We do not ask you to provide sensitive personal information such as your name, postal address, contacts, passwords, private messages, or payment card details as part of the app's core functionality.

3. Local file and app processing

Package extraction, file management, and similar operations are intended to take place locally on your device unless a feature explicitly tells you otherwise.

Files that you select are not automatically uploaded to us simply because you open, extract, manage, or install them through XAPK Manager.

You remain responsible for ensuring that you have the right to extract, copy, share, install, or otherwise use application packages and files processed with the app.

4. Android permissions

XAPK Manager may request Android permissions or system access that are required for specific features. Depending on your Android version and the functionality you use, these can include:

  • access to files or folders that you select;
  • access needed to display information about installed applications;
  • permission to request installation of application packages;
  • notifications or other standard Android permissions required by enabled features.

Permissions are used only for the purposes described by the app and the Android permission prompt. You can review or revoke permissions at any time from Android Settings.

5. Third-party services

XAPK Manager may use third-party services that help distribute, maintain, analyze, monetize, or improve the app. These services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Children's privacy

XAPK Manager is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly request children to submit personal information directly through the app.

7. Data security

We take reasonable measures to limit unnecessary access to information and to use platform security features provided by Android and Google Play. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data retention

Information processed locally by the app generally remains under your control on your device. If third-party services are enabled, their retention practices are governed by their respective privacy policies and service terms.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

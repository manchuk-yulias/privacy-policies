Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains how APK Installer handles information when you use the application.
Last updated: August 11, 2026
Privacy by design

APK and XAPK files selected through APK Installer are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to our own servers.

01

Information We Collect

APK Installer does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

Third-party services integrated into the application may automatically process certain technical information, including:

  • Device information
  • Operating system version
  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • App interactions and usage information
  • Diagnostic and crash information
  • Approximate location derived from network information, where applicable

This information may be used for advertising, analytics, diagnostics, fraud prevention, security, and improving application performance.

02

Files and Applications on Your Device

APK Installer allows you to select and install APK, XAPK, and other supported application package files stored on your device.

Files selected through the application are processed locally on your device for the purpose of performing the requested operation. We do not upload the APK or XAPK files you select to our own servers.

The application may access information about installed applications when necessary to provide features such as application management, package information, sharing, or installation.

03

Advertising

APK Installer may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, including Google.

Advertising providers may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and interactions with advertisements in accordance with their own privacy policies.

08
